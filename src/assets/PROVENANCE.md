# Holystoned deck

`holystoned-deck-v1.png` was created using the built-in image-generation tool on 2 October 2026 for this project. It is an artistic material, not a scan of an authenticated historical deck. Twelve planks are mapped across three metres in the browser; the existing ship dimensions remain sourced separately.

Prompt:

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: seamless square base-color texture for a physically based 3D ship deck, 2048 x 2048. Create a photorealistic flat orthographic overhead material scan of exactly twelve parallel long deck planks running HORIZONTALLY from the left edge to the right edge, filling the entire square. This is scrubbed, salt-weathered, holystoned pale oak from an 1805 Royal Navy frigate. Real fine straight wood fibres, restrained grain, very few small knots, subtle individual board differences, fine charcoal-brown caulk seams between the twelve planks. Several staggered butt joints and tiny wooden treenail plugs near some joints. Muted warm grey tan, neither orange nor white, medium-light albedo. Matte DRY timber; moisture and lighting will be added in the 3D engine. Seamless repeating pattern on all four edges, low contrast without obvious repeating stains. Entire image in focus. Uniform neutral diffuse illumination, no directional light, NO cast shadows, no ambient occlusion baked in, no perspective, no objects, no text, no border, no large black spots. It must look like real photographed naval deck wood, not an illustration or rendered room.

# Blender detail asset

`rigging-deadeye.glb` is generated with Blender by `tools/author-deadeyes.py`.
Its geometry, deterministic elm colour map and baked cavity colours are created
locally by that script. No photograph or external mesh is included. It is an
artistic reconstruction of the period fitting; sources and uncertainties are
in `docs/research/12-deadeyes-and-lanyards.md`. The editable source is saved as
`build/HMS Surprise - Rigging Workshop.blend`.

`quarterdeck-detail.glb` is generated by `tools/author-details.py` via
`node tools/build-authored.mjs`. Fittings, garments, hands, eyes, hair, hats and
surface maps are authored by that script. Wheel and binnacle dimensions are
read from this repository's existing ship specification.

The continuous head topology is adapted from the MakeHuman hm08 CC0 base mesh,
revision `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482`. Only its head surface is used.
The source, original copyright header and full CC0 licence are preserved in
`tools/vendor/makehuman/`. Source and licence:

- https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
- https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482/LICENSE.ASSETS.md

The crew are fictional artistic reconstructions. No named person's likeness is
represented. This GLB is rendered interactively in the browser; it is not an
offline render used as a backdrop.

`quarterdeck-fittings.glb` is a reproducible subset made by
`node tools/extract-fittings.mjs`. It contains only the wheel, its stand and
the binnacle, with the original vertices and materials. Hosts omitting visible
crew can use it without loading facial, garment or hair assets.

# Photographic facial projection

`sailor-face-projection-v1.png` was made with the built-in image generation tool
on 2 October 2026. It depicts a fictional face. The exact input alignment image
is preserved at `tools/assets/portrait-alignment-v1.png`; it was rendered from
the credited CC0 head using `tools/portrait-reference.py`. The output is 1254 ×
1254 pixels. The requested alignment is converted to UV space by the Blender
asset builder and blended into the sides and back of the head. Eyes remain
separate movable geometry. This is an artistic texture, not a historical scan.

Exact prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: front orthographic photographic facial ALBEDO projection texture for an existing 3D sailor model. The attached image is a strict pixel alignment guide, not a style reference. Keep the face, ears, eye centers, nose tip, nostrils, mouth and chin in exactly the same pixel positions and preserve the original head silhouette and scale on the square canvas. Replace the plain synthetic skin with the photographic skin colour of a fictional 40-year-old male sailor in 1805: sun-weathered light-to-medium tan skin, natural pores, faint freckles and sun spots, restrained salt-and-pepper stubble on chin and upper lip, subtle cheek redness, fine crow's feet and forehead creases, slightly chapped natural lips. Replace the sharply triangular painted eyebrows with soft natural hair eyebrows just above the eye sockets, preserving the eye positions. No stylization, no makeup, no idealized beauty. Flat diffuse cross-polarized scan lighting: remove cast shadows and specular highlights; texture detail should come from pigment and skin, not dark lighting. Keep the neutral relaxed expression and mouth closed. Remove the malformed dark hat/hair column above the head and the stray fragments below the chin; finish a natural bald scalp within the existing cranium silhouette. Fill all non-head pixels with a uniform warm medium skin-tan colour, no alpha needed. No neck, shoulders, clothes, text, logo, watermark, border, perspective, framing shift or enlarged face. 1024 by 1024 pixels. This is a technical aligned texture, not a portrait composition.

The browser GLB is compressed by `tools/compress-authored.mjs`. Meshopt encodes
the geometry without a quantization step; the build checks every decoded vertex
position against the original. Base-colour maps use JPEG quality 94 with 4:4:4
chroma; normal and roughness data remain PNG. The uncompressed source is kept
in `build/quarterdeck-detail-uncompressed.glb`, and the editable Blender file
retains its original packed image maps.

# The watch

`crew/seaman.glb`, `crew/oilskin.glb`, `crew/officer.glb` and `crew/midshipman.glb`
were generated with Meshy (meshy.ai) on 15 October 2026: image to 3D (Meshy 7.1),
texturing from the same image, remeshing to about 10,000 triangles, and Meshy's
automatic humanoid rig on its Mixamo skeleton template. They are fictional people;
no named person's likeness is represented. `tools/compress-crew.mjs` turns the
downloads into these files (stock clips removed, four bone weights per vertex,
JPEG maps).

Licences differ by figure:

- `seaman.glb` was generated on Meshy's free plan and is licensed **CC BY 4.0**.
  Attribution: "Seaman figure created with Meshy AI (meshy.ai), CC BY 4.0." Any
  host that shows it should carry that credit.
- `oilskin.glb`, `officer.glb` and `midshipman.glb` were generated on Meshy Pro
  with the Private licence and belong to this project.

Reference images. The seaman's was made with ChatGPT from this prompt:

> Full-body front view of an ordinary seaman of the Royal Navy, about 1805. Standing in a relaxed A-pose, arms held slightly away from the body, legs slightly apart, feet flat. Plain light grey background, soft even studio light, no shadows, no props. He wears a short dark blue wool jacket, open, with plain horn buttons; a loose white linen shirt; a black neckerchief tied loosely; wide white canvas trousers to the ankle, worn and a little dirty; plain black buckled shoes; a low round black tarred hat with a narrow brim. Weathered, tanned man of about 30, stubble, short hair with a short tarred pigtail. Photographic and realistic, natural creases in the cloth. Not a pirate: no sword, no pistols, no eyepatch, no belt of weapons.

The other three were made with Meshy's image tool (Nano Banana Pro, A-pose, 3:4),
each with the seaman's picture as its style reference and these prompts:

> Full-body front view photo of a Royal Navy lieutenant, about 1805, standing in a relaxed A-pose, arms slightly away from the body, feet slightly apart, whole body from hat to shoes in frame. Plain light grey studio background, soft even light, same photographic style as the reference image. Dark navy blue wool tailcoat with stand-up collar, white piping and gilt buttons; white waistcoat; white breeches; white stockings; black buckled shoes; black bicorne cocked hat worn side to side. Weathered clean-shaven man of about 35, hair tied back. Clothes worn and a little salt-stained. No sword, no epaulettes, no props.

> Full-body front view photo of a Royal Navy seaman in foul-weather gear, about 1805, standing in a relaxed A-pose, arms slightly away from the body, feet slightly apart, whole body from hat to shoes in frame. Plain light grey studio background, soft even light, same photographic style as the reference image. Long tarred canvas coat to mid-thigh, stiff, dark brown-black with a wet sheen, buttoned to the neck; grey knitted wool scarf; wide canvas trousers soaked dark grey; plain black shoes; low black tarred round hat with a brim, tied under the chin. Weathered man of about 40, stubble, rain-soaked face. No props, no weapons.

> Full-body front view photo of a Royal Navy midshipman, about 1805, a slim boy of about 15, standing in a relaxed A-pose, arms slightly away from the body, feet slightly apart, whole body from hat to shoes in frame. Plain light grey studio background, soft even light, same photographic style as the reference image. Short dark navy blue tailcoat with small white patches on the collar and gilt buttons; white waistcoat; white trousers to the ankle; plain black shoes; black round hat with a narrow brim. Freckled, short brown hair, wind-reddened cheeks. Clothes worn and slightly dirty. No sword, no dirk, no props.
